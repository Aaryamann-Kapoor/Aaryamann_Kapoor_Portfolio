About Me

Crafting intelligent systems with purpose.

I'm an AI/ML student and developer who enjoys building practical, real-world technology with a creative edge. My interests span artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, full-stack development, and robotics.

I've worked on projects like WattWise — a campus energy intelligence dashboard — and Robotic Grasp Learning via Reinforcement Learning, using PyBullet, SAC, and PPO in simulation environments.

I was selected for the ChatGPT Student Lab India cohort, collaborating with students nationwide to explore how AI improves learning and productivity.

Beyond code, I'm drawn to design, startups, leadership, and products that feel polished and meaningful. My goal is technology that's not just functional — but impactful.
