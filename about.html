About me

A student who builds things
to understand them better.

I'm Aaryamann Kapoor, a second-year B.Tech student specialising in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at Delhi Technical Campus (batch 2024–2028). I'm currently sitting at a SGPA of 9.13, but honestly the number matters less to me than what I've been able to build along the way.

My interest in AI started as curiosity about how machines can learn from data — and quickly turned into hands-on projects. I've built an energy intelligence platform that combines live ESP8266 sensor telemetry with LSTM and XGBoost forecasting models, worked on business entity resolution for anti-money-laundering use cases, and explored disaster management systems that try to make emergency response smarter.

I was selected for the ChatGPT Student Lab — India Cohort, collaborating with students across the country on how AI can improve learning and productivity. That experience reinforced something I already believed: the best technology is technology that solves real problems clearly, not one that shows off.

Outside of coursework and projects, I'm drawn to the product and design side of things — thinking about why something works (or doesn't), and what it would take to make it genuinely useful. I'm also an active participant in hackathons, where the constraint of building fast tends to produce some of the most honest thinking.

If you're working on something interesting in AI, energy systems, or data engineering — or just want to talk — I'm easy to reach.
